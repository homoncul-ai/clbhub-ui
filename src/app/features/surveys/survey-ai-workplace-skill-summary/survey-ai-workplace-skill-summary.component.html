AI Workplace Skill Summary

Understanding baseline digital readiness for new graduates

We're collecting employer perspectives on baseline digital and adaptive skills. Your responses will help educators, workforce programs, and community partners align training with what hiring managers actually expect on Day 1.

Artificial intelligence is changing how work gets done across every sector. As tools like generative AI become part of daily workflows, employers need graduates who can use them productively — and verify outputs responsibly.

This short survey asks about your industry, the baseline skills you consider essential for entry-level hires, how prepared recent graduates are today, the supervision you expect during onboarding, and how AI proficiency affects your hiring decisions.

What you'll answer

  • Part 1 — Industry & demographics
  • Part 2 — Core readiness & graduate comparisons
  • Part 3 — Performance & onboarding expectations
  • Part 4 — Non-traditional job seekers
  • Part 5 — Market value of AI proficiency

The survey takes about 5–10 minutes. You can move freely between steps and review your answers before submitting.
