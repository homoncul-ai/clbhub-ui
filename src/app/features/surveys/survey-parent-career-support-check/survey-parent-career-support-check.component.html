Parent Career Support Check

Understanding how parents and guardians support young people's career exploration

Your responses will help us better understand the needs, challenges, and priorities of parents as we continue developing CLBHub. By learning what information families need, how they prefer to communicate, and where they need the most support, we can focus on building the resources, tools, and opportunities that matter most to parents and the young people they support.

Parents and guardians play an important role in helping young people explore careers, education, and training opportunities. This survey is designed to better understand how families support career exploration, what resources they use, how confident they feel guiding young people, and how organizations can better communicate with and support families.

What you'll answer

  • About you and the young person
  • Your involvement in career exploration
  • Youth's career exploration
  • Your confidence supporting career exploration
  • Technology & AI
  • Your ideas and feedback
  • Follow-up
