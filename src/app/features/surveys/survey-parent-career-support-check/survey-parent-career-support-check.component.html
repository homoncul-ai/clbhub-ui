Understanding how parents and guardians support young people’s career exploration

Your answers will help educators, youth programs, community organizations, and workforce partners design supports that make career exploration more accessible and useful for families. This survey helps us understand how families support career exploration, what resources they use, and how confident they feel guiding young people.

Parents and guardians play an important role in helping young people explore careers, education, and training opportunities. This short survey is designed to better understand how families support career exploration, what resources they use, and how organizations can better communicate with and support families.

The survey takes about 5–10 minutes. You can move freely between steps and review your answers before submitting.

What you'll answer

  • About you and the young person
  • Your involvement in career exploration
  • How the young person explores careers and uses AI
  • Your confidence supporting career exploration
  • Your communication preferences and feedback
