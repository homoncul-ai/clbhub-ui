Youth Career Check

Understanding how young people explore careers, use AI, and engage with support networks

Your answers will help educators, youth programs, and community partners shape early pilot activities. This survey helps us understand how young people search for jobs and training, who supports them, and whether cohort-style programs could help.

Career exploration looks different for everyone. This short survey is designed to understand how young people search for jobs and training, who helps them, how much effort they put in, and whether a small cohort-style program would help.

The survey takes about 5–10 minutes. You can move freely between steps and review your answers before submitting.

What you'll answer

  • About you
  • How you explore careers
  • How you use AI in career exploration
  • Who supports your career search
  • Your interest in cohort-style activities and feedback
