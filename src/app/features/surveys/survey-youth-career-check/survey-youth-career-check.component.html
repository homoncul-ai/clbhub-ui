Youth Career Exploration

Youth Career Check

Young people are at the center of exploring careers, education, training, and future opportunities.

Your responses will help us better understand the needs, interests, challenges, and priorities of young people as we continue developing CLBHub. By learning how you explore careers, what information and opportunities you're looking for, and what support would be most helpful, we can focus on building the resources, tools, and community experiences that matter most to you.

This survey is designed to better understand how young people explore career options, what resources they use, who supports them, how they use technology, and what kinds of tools, opportunities, and programs would best support their career exploration.

What you’ll answer

  • Page 1 – About you
  • Page 2 – How you explore careers
  • Page 3 – Who is involved in your career search
  • Page 4 – How often and how long you plan
  • Page 5 – Interest in cohort-style activities
  • Page 6 – Your ideas and feedback

This survey takes about 5–10 minutes. You can move freely between pages and review your responses before submitting.
