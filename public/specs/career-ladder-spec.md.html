[← Specs index](./) # Career Ladder Spec JSON and bean model for creating **CareerLadder** and **CareerLadderRung** content, plus how a learner progresses through a ladder in the system. --- ## Reference beans (setup / catalog) | # | Bean | Role | |---|------|------| | 1 | **CareerLadderRef** | Reference for a career ladder (e.g. healthcare-aide). | | 2 | **CareerLadderRungRef** | A rung on a ladder. Links to an action-group definition of what that rung asks of the learner. | | 3 | **PActionGroupRef** | Aspiration / plan template — e.g. “get a job as a CNA.” Like a test plan: what we’re going to do. | | 4 | **PActionDetailRef** | A concrete thing you can sign up for and be evaluated on (entry + completion). Master/detail to **PLinkedQualifier**. Examples: babysit, work on a job site, complete a training program. | | 5 | **PLinkedQualifier** | Links a **PActionDetailRef** to a **QualifierRef**. Each goal has requirements / outcomes. | | 6 | **QualifierRef** | A requirement or qualification that can appear on a resume. Examples: GED, a certification, lift 50 lbs, town residency, Over18, merit badge. | | 7 | **QualifierTypeRef** | Type of qualifier: Education, Certification, Intrinsic, Misc, etc. | ### PLinkedQualifier types - **Requirement** — Needed before starting or applying (used to gauge suitability). - **Earned** — Qualification gained once the learner finishes the activity (class, experience, exam). --- ## Instance beans (per learner) | # | Bean | Example | |---|------|---------| | 1 | **PActionGroupInstance** | Yvonne wants to become a day-care specialist, level 1. | | 2 | **PActionDetailInstance** | Yvonne will babysit — the detail instance carries a score. | | 3 | **PLinkedQualifierInstance** | Yvonne’s readiness for “likes kids,” readiness for first aid. | ### Related beans **PQualification** — Links a **QualifierRef**, type, and experience to a user. - Rocco earned his GED via Experience:GED Test at HHS — summer 2025 (notes, optional approver). - Default facts (e.g. Rocco is over 18). - Used for gates: can Rocco take a college-level course? Work as a store clerk at night? **Experience** — Concrete offering that can award qualifiers. - Samantha Watson taught an AI cohort over the summer. - Learners who complete that class receive a GED-related certificate (example of Experience → Qualifier). --- ## How beans fit together ```text CareerLadderRef └── CareerLadderRungRef[] (ordered rungs) └── PActionGroupRef (what this rung asks you to do) └── PActionDetailRef[] (sign-up activities) └── PLinkedQualifier[] → QualifierRef (+ QualifierTypeRef) ├── Requirement └── Earned Per learner: PActionGroupInstance └── PActionDetailInstance[] └── PLinkedQualifierInstance[] PQualification[] (earned / attested quals on the person) ``` Public API shape (for import / browse) maps roughly to: - Ladder → **CareerLadderRef** (+ nested rung summaries) - Rung → **CareerLadderRungRef** (+ requirements, skills, certifications as **QualifierRef** / linked content) - Certifications / tutorials → catalog of **QualifierRef** / **PActionDetailRef**-style offerings --- ## JSON example — rung Maps to **CareerLadderRungRef** content (plus linked certifications as **QualifierRef**). ```json { "slug": "cna", "title": "Certified Nursing Assistant (CNA)", "shortTitle": "CNA", "oneLiner": "Pass a 75–100 hour MA-approved CNA program — work in nursing homes, hospitals, and rehabs.", "summary": "A CNA is a state-registered aide who works under the direction of a nurse, mostly in nursing homes, rehabs, and hospitals. She opens the **healthcare-aide** ladder (**CareerLadderRef**) and browses rungs. The **home-health-aide** rung feels reachable; the **cna** rung looks like a stretch. She “starts” the HHA/PCA rung. The system creates a **PActionGroupInstance** aimed at that rung’s **PActionGroupRef**. Under it she sees concrete next steps as **PActionDetailRef** items — e.g. a short first-aid workshop and a supervised babysitting / companion shift. She signs up for babysitting; that creates a **PActionDetailInstance**. Before she can start, the system checks **PLinkedQualifier** rows of type **Requirement** (e.g. Over18 may be waived for school-sponsored babysitting; “likes working with people” readiness). Her counselor marks readiness on a **PLinkedQualifierInstance**. After a few scored babysitting sessions, her **PActionDetailInstance** score improves. Completing CPR awards a **PQualification** linked to **QualifierRef** `cpr-fa` (type **Earned**), which later unlocks CNA clinicals. In product terms: browse ladder → pick a rung → get a personal action plan → complete small activities → accumulate qualifiers → see the next rung light up. ### Narrative 2 — Rocco: climbing with quals and an employer-paid program Rocco already works nights as a store clerk and has a GED on file as a **PQualification** (from Experience:GED Test at HHS). He opens the same ladder, skips HHA, and targets **cna**. The CNA rung’s **PActionGroupRef** lists details: enroll in a DPH-approved program, pass the competency exam, land on the registry. Requirement **PLinkedQualifier**s ask for HS/GED, age 16+, and ideally CPR. His GED **PQualification** satisfies the education gate; he still needs CPR, so the UI surfaces a **PActionDetailRef** for a weekend BLS class. He uses the research section to find a DPH-approved program, and signs up. Completing the program + exam creates **PQualification**s for `ma-cna` (and updates readiness **PLinkedQualifierInstance**s). His **PActionGroupInstance** for the CNA rung moves to complete. The ladder UI advances his “current rung” toward **lpn**, showing remaining requirements (NCLEX path, time-on-job) without inventing progress he hasn’t earned. In product terms: existing quals short-circuit requirements → learner commits to a rung → experiences and employer programs feed **PQualification** → completion of the group instance is the signal that the rung is done and the next one is in play.
