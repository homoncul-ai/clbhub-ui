[← Specs index](./) # CLBHub Cohort and Cohort Experience Model ## Purpose In CLBHub, a **cohort** is a durable, leader-guided career community: analogous to a group of students in the same major or a shared career pathway. Cohort members are called **participants**. A cohort is not a single course, event, or fixed-duration class. It provides long-term identity, strategic guidance, team membership, discussion, and a curated set of career-relevant opportunities. Participants may remain in a cohort until they leave, complete its progression criteria, or are explicitly transferred to another cohort or career-ladder level. A cohort can have one or more associated **Cohort Experiences**. A Cohort Experience associates a cohort with a reusable Experience and decorates it with cohort-specific context. This document defines the cohort-side association, participant relationship, and roundtable requirement. The separate Experience specification owns the mechanics of creating, scheduling, operating, communicating about, registering for, and completing the underlying Experience. ## Core principle > A cohort is a leader-guided career community that curates a pathway of opportunities. A Cohort Experience places a particular Experience in that cohort's strategic context and records the cohort participants connected to it. The responsibility boundary is deliberate: - **Cohort:** Long-lived, strategic membership, career guidance, team, and overarching goals. - **Experience:** The operational activity. It owns the mechanics of running the event or activity. - **Cohort Experience:** The association between a Cohort and an Experience, decorated with cohort-specific details, participant relationships, and optional post-experience roundtable requirements. - **Cohort Experience Participant:** A participant's relationship to a specific Cohort Experience, including cohort-specific sponsorship information. - **Career Ladder:** The organizing framework that gives cohorts career direction, levels, competencies, suggested experiences, and progression guidance. ## Scope and non-goals This document is intentionally limited to Cohort Experience behavior. ### In scope - Associating one or more Experiences with a Cohort. - Cohort-specific display and contextual fields for an associated Experience. - The `CohortExperienceParticipant` relationship. - The requirement for a post-experience roundtable. - Cohort calendar presentation. - Cohort-side examples and lifecycle expectations. ### Out of scope The **Experience** specification is separate and owns: - Experience creation and catalog behavior. - Scheduling, locations, agendas, capacity, and event occurrences. - Registration, waitlists, invitations, public visibility, and public sign-up. - Attendance and check-in. - Experience roles, operational staff, chaperones, speakers, and logistics. - Experience-level chat, materials, tasks, tactical goals, and operational communications. - The detailed implementation of roundtables, questions, responses, moderation, and reporting. This document may refer to those capabilities only to define the integration boundary. ## Conceptual model ```text Career Ladder └── Cohort ├── Cohort Team ├── Cohort Leader(s) ├── Cohort Group Chat ├── Strategic Goals and Guidance ├── CohortExperience │ ├── points to Experience │ ├── cohort-specific title and description │ ├── importance │ ├── roundtable requirement │ └── CohortExperienceParticipant │ └── points to Participant │ ├── CohortExperience │ └── points to another Experience │ └── Cohort membership and transfer history ``` A Cohort Experience is not a duplicate of an Experience. It is a cohort-specific association that enables the same underlying Experience to be related to multiple cohorts with different context, display text, importance, participant associations, and reflection/roundtable expectations. ## Example A cohort can arrange or feature a sequence of experiences across a period of time: ```text Cohort: Advanced Manufacturing Explorers — Level 1 Week 1 CohortExperience: Industry Speaker Week Experience: A Day in the Life of a CNC Machinist Cohort context: Important introductory exposure to manufacturing careers Importance: 5 Roundtable required: Yes Week 2 CohortExperience: Museum Exploration Experience: Museum of Science Engineering Exhibit Trip Cohort context: Connects the cohort's career exploration goals to hands-on examples Importance: 4 Roundtable required: Yes ``` The Experience records handle the actual speaker activity and museum trip mechanics. The Cohort Experience records explain why each Experience matters to this cohort and what cohort-level follow-up is expected. ## Entities ### Cohort `Cohort` is assumed to already exist. No changes to the Cohort entity are required by this specification. The Cohort is the parent of one or more `CohortExperience` records. ```text Cohort 1 ──── * CohortExperience ``` ### Experience `Experience` is assumed to exist and is specified elsewhere. No changes to the Experience entity are defined here. A `CohortExperience` points to one Experience. ```text Experience 1 ──── * CohortExperience ``` This cardinality allows a single reusable Experience to be associated with multiple Cohorts. ### CohortExperience `CohortExperience` associates one Cohort with one Experience and decorates that relationship with cohort-specific details. ```text CohortExperience - id - cohortId // required; points to Cohort - experienceId // required; points to Experience - title // cohort-calendar title; defaults to Experience title - description // how/why the Experience pertains to this Cohort - importance // integer from 0 through 5; 5 is very important - roundtableRequired // whether CohortExperienceParticipants must complete a roundtable, if applicable ``` #### Field definitions | Field | Type | Required | Description | |---|---:|---:|---| | `id` | identifier | Yes | Unique identity for the Cohort Experience association | | `cohortId` | identifier | Yes | The Cohort to which this Experience is associated | | `experienceId` | identifier | Yes | The reusable Experience associated with this Cohort | | `title` | string | Yes | The title shown on the cohort calendar; defaults to the associated Experience title when created | | `description` | text | No | Explains how the Experience pertains to, supports, or advances this Cohort's strategic purpose | | `importance` | integer | Yes | Cohort-specific importance from 0 to 5, where 5 means very important | | `roundtableRequired` | boolean | Yes | Indicates whether each Cohort Experience Participant is required to answer roundtable questions after the Experience completes | #### Title behavior When a Cohort Experience is created, its `title` should default to the associated Experience title. The cohort leader or authorized user may override it to make its meaning clear in the cohort calendar. Examples: | Experience title | Cohort Experience title | |---|---| | A Day in the Life of a CNC Machinist | Industry Speaker Week: CNC Machinist | | Museum of Science Engineering Exhibit Trip | Week 2: Engineering Museum Exploration | | Intro to CAD Workshop | Recommended CAD Preparation Workshop | Changing the title on a Cohort Experience should not change the canonical title of the underlying Experience. #### Description behavior The `description` is cohort-specific context, not a restatement of the Experience description. Examples: - “Introduces participants to a local manufacturing career pathway and supports the cohort's career-exploration goal.” - “A recommended follow-up for participants considering robotics or mechanical design roles.” - “This visit provides examples for the cohort's next roundtable discussion on engineering career interests.” #### Importance `importance` is an integer constrained to `0..5`: | Value | Suggested meaning | |---:|---| | 0 | Informational or optional reference | | 1 | Low priority | | 2 | Helpful | | 3 | Recommended | | 4 | Highly recommended | | 5 | Very important / major cohort priority | Importance is a guidance and presentation attribute. It does not by itself create registration, attendance, completion, or progression requirements. #### Roundtable requirement `roundtableRequired` expresses a cohort-specific follow-up requirement. When it is `true`, each relevant `CohortExperienceParticipant` must answer the configured roundtable questions after the Experience has completed, subject to the detailed rules in the separate Experience/Roundtable specification. The Cohort Experience does not define the full roundtable mechanism in this document. It only declares that the cohort requires it. Potential downstream outcomes include: - Marking a participant's cohort-side follow-up as pending. - Prompting the participant after Experience completion. - Allowing a cohort leader to see who has or has not completed the required roundtable. - Using responses as evidence for strategic cohort or Career Ladder progress. ### CohortExperienceParticipant `CohortExperienceParticipant` associates a Participant with a specific Cohort Experience. ```text CohortExperienceParticipant - id - cohortExperienceId // required; points to CohortExperience - participantId // required; points to Participant - sponsoredByOrgId // organization that sponsored the participant, if applicable ``` #### Field definitions | Field | Type | Required | Description | |---|---:|---:|---| | `id` | identifier | Yes | Unique identity for this participant's Cohort Experience association | | `cohortExperienceId` | identifier | Yes | The associated Cohort Experience | | `participantId` | identifier | Yes | The Participant connected to this Cohort Experience | | `sponsoredByOrgId` | identifier | No | The Organization that sponsored this participant in connection with this Cohort Experience | `CohortExperienceParticipant` is distinct from any participant record that belongs to the underlying Experience. The Experience specification remains authoritative for operational enrollment, registration, role, attendance, and check-in. This cohort-side record exists to express the participant's cohort context and sponsorship information. ### Relationship diagram ```text Cohort 1 ──── * CohortExperience * ──── 1 Experience 1 ──── * CohortExperienceParticipant * ──── 1 Participant * ──── 0..1 Organization (sponsoredByOrgId) ``` ## Cohort Experience lifecycle The Experience lifecycle is defined elsewhere. The Cohort Experience follows that underlying lifecycle for display and cohort-side follow-up. ### Creation A cohort leader or authorized user: 1. Selects an existing Experience. 2. Creates a `CohortExperience` that points to the Cohort and selected Experience. 3. Uses the Experience title as the default Cohort Experience title. 4. Adds or edits the cohort-specific title and description. 5. Sets `importance` from 0 to 5. 6. Determines whether `roundtableRequired` is true or false. 7. Associates relevant participants as `CohortExperienceParticipant` records, as appropriate. The association may be created before participants are connected to it. Participant selection and operational registration remain separate concerns. ### During the Experience All operational mechanics are managed through the Experience: - Schedule and agenda. - Public listing or private access. - Registration and enrollment. - Attendance and check-in. - Operational participant roles. - Materials and messages. - Chat and activity logistics. The cohort calendar presents the Cohort Experience using its cohort-specific `title`, `description`, and `importance` while linking to the underlying Experience for operational details. ### Completion and roundtable follow-up After the underlying Experience completes: 1. The system identifies the relevant `CohortExperienceParticipant` records. 2. If `roundtableRequired` is `false`, no cohort-specific roundtable follow-up is required. 3. If `roundtableRequired` is `true`, each Cohort Experience Participant must answer the roundtable questions for that Cohort Experience, subject to the detailed roundtable rules defined elsewhere. 4. The system can present outstanding roundtable work in participant and cohort-leader views. 5. Authorized leaders can use completion and responses as evidence of strategic cohort progress. The required roundtable is cohort-specific. The same underlying Experience may be linked to another Cohort with a different title, importance, description, or no roundtable requirement. ## Calendar behavior The cohort calendar should show each `CohortExperience` in the cohort's strategic context. For a Cohort Experience calendar entry, show: - Cohort Experience title. - Underlying Experience date/time, as supplied by the Experience. - Cohort Experience description. - Importance indicator. - Roundtable-required indicator when applicable. - Link to the underlying Experience for operational detail and participation mechanics. The calendar should not duplicate or become the source of truth for the Experience's scheduling and operations. ## Examples ### Speaker Week ```text CohortExperience - cohortId: advanced-manufacturing-explorers-level-1 - experienceId: cnc-machinist-speaker-session - title: Industry Speaker Week: CNC Machinist - description: Introduces participants to a local manufacturing career pathway and supports the cohort's exploration goal. - importance: 5 - roundtableRequired: true ``` ```text CohortExperienceParticipant - cohortExperienceId: industry-speaker-week-cnc - participantId: participant-123 - sponsoredByOrgId: beverly-public-schools ``` ### Museum Visit ```text CohortExperience - cohortId: advanced-manufacturing-explorers-level-1 - experienceId: museum-science-engineering-trip - title: Week 2: Engineering Museum Exploration - description: Connects career interests identified in Speaker Week to hands-on engineering and technology exhibits. - importance: 4 - roundtableRequired: true ``` ### Shared Experience, different cohort context The same Experience can be used by different cohorts without duplication. ```text Experience: Regional Robotics Career Panel CohortExperience A - Cohort: Robotics Explorers - title: Robotics Career Panel - importance: 5 - description: Core exploration activity for participants considering robotics careers. - roundtableRequired: true CohortExperience B - Cohort: Advanced Manufacturing Explorers - title: Optional Robotics Career Panel - importance: 2 - description: An elective opportunity for participants interested in automation and controls. - roundtableRequired: false ``` ## Data integrity and implementation rules - `cohortId` and `experienceId` are required for every Cohort Experience. - `importance` must be an integer between 0 and 5, inclusive. - `title` defaults from the Experience title at creation time but is stored independently thereafter. - Changing a Cohort Experience title or description must not change the underlying Experience. - A Cohort Experience must not duplicate Experience operational data such as schedule, capacity, registration, attendance, chat, or materials. - The Experience is authoritative for operational participation and attendance. - `CohortExperienceParticipant` must reference an existing Cohort Experience and Participant. - `sponsoredByOrgId` is optional and must reference an existing Organization if supplied. - A participant may be associated with more than one Cohort Experience. - The same Experience may be associated with multiple Cohorts through multiple Cohort Experience records. - The same underlying Experience may have different cohort-specific titles, descriptions, importance levels, and roundtable requirements. - A Cohort Experience should not automatically imply that every cohort participant is operationally registered for the Experience. - A Cohort Experience Participant should not be assumed to be checked in or to have attended; attendance remains owned by the Experience. ## Open decisions The following decisions are intentionally left TBD or belong in the separate Experience/Roundtable specification: - Which cohort-specific details beyond title, description, importance, and roundtable requirement belong on `CohortExperience`. - Whether a Cohort Experience can be created from only catalog-backed Experiences or from any existing Experience. - Whether a Cohort Experience can associate all current cohort members in bulk. - Whether `CohortExperienceParticipant` is automatically created from operational Experience registration, manually selected, or both. - Whether a Cohort Experience Participant must be an active member of the Cohort at the time of association. - Whether `sponsoredByOrgId` is a snapshot for historical reporting or reflects a current sponsorship relationship. - The exact behavior, question set, response model, deadlines, moderation, and reporting of a required roundtable. - Whether roundtable completion is required for all Cohort Experience Participants or only those who attended the underlying Experience. - Whether a failed/no-show operational participation can exempt, block, or still require a roundtable. - How Cohort Experience completion and roundtable responses contribute to Career Ladder or Cohort progress. - Whether a Cohort Experience can have more than one underlying Experience. ## Product statement > In CLBHub, a Cohort Experience associates a strategic cohort with an operational Experience. It decorates that association with cohort-specific context, calendar presentation, importance, participant sponsorship, and an optional requirement for participants to complete a post-experience roundtable.
