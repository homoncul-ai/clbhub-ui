# Data Usage and Retention Policy **PLEASE READ THIS POLICY CAREFULLY.** This Data Usage and Retention Policy (“**Policy**”) describes how CLBHub.org collects, uses, retains, and deletes personal information through `www.clbhub.org`, `hav.clbhub.org`, and related CLBHub.org services (collectively, the **“Site”**). References to **“us,” “we,” “our,” or “CLBHub.org”** mean CLBHub.org and its affiliates. References to **“you,” “your,” or “User”** mean any person who uses the Site, including students, parents/guardians, educators, school and district staff, employers, nonprofit staff, survey participants, and public visitors. This Policy supplements our Terms of Use and any applicable privacy statements, institutional agreements, and consent notices. Where those documents conflict with this Policy on data sale, surveillance, or deletion requests, the more protective provision for you applies. --- ## 1) Our Purpose CLBHub.org exists to help people. We build tools for student growth, career exploration, opportunity matching, school and community collaboration, and workforce readiness. We use data only to support those human-centered goals: connecting people with education, training, jobs, mentors, and community resources. **We are not in the business of surveillance.** We do not design our platform to track people for unrelated commercial profiling, political manipulation, or mass monitoring. We do not sell personal information, and we do not intend to become part of a surveillance economy. --- ## 2) What We Collect Depending on how you use the Site, we may collect: - account and profile information (such as name, email, role, and organization); - educational, career, and opportunity-related information you choose to provide; - communications sent through platform messaging or support channels; - survey, check-in, and event responses you submit; - technical and security logs (such as IP address, browser type, and access timestamps); - usage information needed to operate, secure, and improve the Site. We ask you **not** to submit sensitive personal information unless a specific workflow clearly requires it and you have chosen to provide it. Schools, districts, and partner organizations may define additional local rules for what may be collected in their environments. --- ## 3) How We Use Data We use personal information only for legitimate platform purposes, including: - providing accounts, profiles, and requested services; - matching users with opportunities, programs, and support resources; - enabling communication between authorized participants; - operating surveys, reporting, and feedback workflows; - securing the Site and preventing fraud or abuse; - complying with law and responding to lawful requests; - improving product quality, accessibility, and reliability. We may create **aggregated or de-identified** statistics that cannot reasonably identify an individual. We use those statistics for reporting, research, and service improvement. We do not attempt to re-identify de-identified data. We do **not** use personal information for unrelated targeted advertising, covert behavioral profiling, or sale to data brokers. --- ## 4) We Do Not Sell Your Data **CLBHub.org does not sell personal information.** For clarity: - we do not sell, rent, lease, or trade personal information for money; - we do not sell personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising; - we do not provide personal information to data brokers for their independent commercial use; - we do not monetize user activity through undisclosed third-party data marketplaces. If applicable law defines “sale” or “sharing” differently, we commit to honoring the spirit of this section: **your personal information is not a product we offer for purchase by others.** We may use service providers (such as hosting, email, analytics, or security vendors) who process data on our behalf under contractual restrictions. Those providers may use the data only to perform services for us, not for their own independent commercial exploitation of your personal information. --- ## 5) No Surveillance Business Model We reject uses of personal data that treat people as inventory. Accordingly, we do not: - build secret profiles for unrelated commercial exploitation; - enable undisclosed monitoring of users beyond what is needed to operate the Site; - share personal information with third parties for their independent surveillance products; - use the platform to compile dossiers for unrelated government, political, or commercial monitoring. Operational logging, fraud prevention, and security monitoring may still occur where reasonably necessary to protect users and the Site. Those activities are limited, purpose-bound, and not used as a pretext for unrelated tracking. Our goal is to strengthen opportunity and community support—not to expand digital surveillance. --- ## 6) Retention We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to: - provide the services you request; - maintain security, audit trails, and backup integrity; - meet contractual obligations with schools and partner organizations; - comply with applicable law; and - resolve disputes or enforce our agreements. Retention periods may vary by data type, account status, and institutional settings. When retention is no longer required for the purposes above, we delete or de-identify the information in accordance with this Policy and our technical capabilities. Backup systems may retain deleted data for a limited period before overwrite. During that window, deleted information is not used for ordinary product operations. --- ## 7) Your Right to Deletion **You may request deletion of your personal information at any time.** To request deletion, contact us using the channels listed in Section 11. Please include enough information for us to verify your identity and locate your account or submission. When we receive a valid deletion request, we will: 1. confirm receipt within a reasonable time; 2. delete or de-identify personal information we control, unless an exception applies; and 3. notify you when deletion is complete or explain any limited retention required by law. Deletion may include account closure, removal of profile content, and removal of identifiable survey or submission records where technically feasible. ### Exceptions We may retain limited information when deletion is not immediately possible because of: - legal obligations or valid legal process; - security, fraud prevention, or abuse investigation needs; - contractual requirements with a school or partner organization that administers your account; - technical constraints in backup or disaster-recovery systems for a limited retention window. Where retention is required, we restrict use of retained data to the purpose that required retention and delete it when that purpose ends. If your account is managed by a school, district, or partner organization, deletion requests may need to be coordinated with that organization’s administrator under its policies and applicable law. --- ## 8) Surveys, Events, and Research Responses Survey, check-in, and event data are collected to understand community needs, measure program quality, and improve services. Unless you are told otherwise at the point of collection: - responses are used for program planning, reporting, and product improvement; - identifiable responses are not sold; - aggregated or de-identified results may be shared with partners for community benefit. You may request deletion of identifiable survey or event submissions you provided, subject to the exceptions in Section 7. --- ## 9) Institutional and Partner Context When a school, district, employer, or nonprofit administers CLBHub.org for its members, that organization may control certain account and roster data. In those cases: - the organization may define local retention and access rules; - deletion requests may require administrator involvement; and - CLBHub.org will cooperate with lawful institutional direction while honoring this Policy’s commitments on non-sale and user-requested deletion where applicable. --- ## 10) Security We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If you believe your account or data has been compromised, contact us promptly. --- ## 11) Contact and Deletion Requests For questions about this Policy, data usage, or deletion requests, contact: **CLBHub.org Privacy & Data Requests** Email: `privacy@clbhub.org` Please include: - your name and contact email; - the Site or organization context (if applicable); - a description of the data you want deleted; and - enough information for us to verify your identity. We may request additional verification before processing sensitive requests. --- ## 12) Children and Minors Where minors use the Site, schools, guardians, and applicable law may require additional consent and supervision. We do not sell minors’ personal information. Deletion requests for minor accounts may be submitted by the account holder, guardian, or authorized school administrator, as applicable. --- ## 13) Changes to This Policy We may update this Policy from time to time. The revised version will be posted on the Site with an updated effective date. Material changes will be communicated where required by law. Continued use of the Site after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy, except where applicable law requires additional consent. --- ## 14) Summary of Our Commitments In plain language: - **We help people.** Data supports education, careers, and community opportunity. - **We do not sell your data.** Personal information is not merchandise. - **We reject surveillance capitalism.** We do not build our business on covert tracking. - **You can ask us to delete your data.** We will honor valid deletion requests, subject to limited legal and operational exceptions. --- **CLBHub.org — Data Usage and Retention Policy (Effective June 2026).**
