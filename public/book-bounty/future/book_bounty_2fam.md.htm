# Book Bounty Program – BBP ## Rules - List every book you read. No entry, no $. - Momina and Adam in charge. - Disputes resolved by Adam (problems). - Max 3 books per week. - Never read same book twice. - Books must be at grade level or above. - Adam decides on amount to pay out. - No credit for books read in school ## Amount - $1 – Read a book to a younger brother or sister - Limit 3 per week - Must be listed in chart - $1 – Read a book at your grade level - $2 – Read a book > 50 pages at or above your grade level - $3 – Read a > 100 page book at grade - $4 – Read a book > 100 pages above grade level > “>” means more than. ## Extras - $5 per week to Momina for keeping the list for her family. - $5 per week for Momina to keep records for Rajat's family. **Bounty ends if > 2 weeks no activity.**
