# CLBHub Haverhill Summer Jobs MVP Document **CLBHub Haverhill** connects Haverhill students with summer jobs, internships, classes, and camps via a vocational-focused platform. The MVP targets a *summer 2026 launch*, integrating with Haverhill Helps, to maximize student placements while tracking metrics for iteration. Providers (businesses/nonprofits/schools) manage opportunities; families/students browse and apply. Core value: Frictionless matching prioritizing transit access, travel time, and student skills/interests, with admin oversight for quality. ## Goals and Success Metrics **Primary Goal**: Place 100+ Haverhill students in summer opportunities by August 2026. **Key Metrics**: | Metric | Target | Tracking Method | |-------------------|-----------|--------------------| | Student signups | 500+ | Dashboard count | | Opportunities listed | 200+ | Provider catalog | | Placement rate | 20%+ | Hired/accepted status | | Completion rate | 80%+ | End-of-summer check-ins | | Feedback NPS | 7+ | Post-placement surveys | | Provider retention| 70%+ | Returning listings | Feedback loops enable yearly improvements; all roles can submit via simple forms. ## Platform Components The CLBHub ecosystem consists of three major elements working together: ### clbhub.org - Public Website The public-facing marketing and signup portal for CLBHub. This site will be used for marketing and promotions for students, families, providers, and community partners. * **Purpose**: Introduce CLBHub to students, families, providers, and community partners * **Features**: - Overview of the program, mission, and benefits - Provider signup and onboarding initiation - Family/student registration flow - FAQ, contact information, and support resources - Links to Haverhill Helps and partner organizations * **Access**: Open to all; no authentication required to browse ### hav.clbhub.org - Haverhill Application Instance The full-featured web application managing all Haverhill-specific workflows. * **Purpose**: Operational hub for all authenticated users to manage opportunities, applications, and placements * **Features**: - Role-based dashboards (Admin, Provider, Student, Family, Counselor) - Catalog management and opportunity listings - Application submission and tracking - Messaging and communication center - Metrics dashboards and reporting - Map-based opportunity discovery with transit/proximity filters * **Access**: Authenticated users only; role-based permissions * **Tech**: Mobile-first responsive web app (Angular/Java stack) * **Communication**: All messaging and notifications happen in-app; email used only for account verification and password resets ### CLBHub Mobile App (Student) A lightweight mobile app focused on core student workflows. * **Purpose**: Give students quick, on-the-go access to browse and apply for opportunities * **MVP Features**: - Login via existing CLBHub credentials - Map-based opportunity feed with filters (distance, duration, paid/unpaid) - View opportunity details and provider info - Submit applications directly from the app - Track application status and receive push notifications - Message providers within opportunity context * **Future Enhancements**: Resume builder, personal statement editor, feedback surveys * **Platforms**: iOS and Android (consider React Native or Flutter for cross-platform) --- ## User Roles and Permissions Two classes: **App Users** (providers/families/students) and **Ecosystem Managers** (admins/counselors). | Role | Key Actions | Permissions | |-----------------------|---------------------------------------|------------------------------------| | **Families/Parents** | Onboard students;
