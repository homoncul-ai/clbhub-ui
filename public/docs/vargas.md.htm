# Andy Vargas ## Overview **Andy Vargas** serves as a Democratic state representative for Massachusetts' 3rd Essex District, which includes part of Haverhill. He chairs the Joint Committee on Community Development and Small Businesses. ## Background Born to Dominican immigrant parents in Haverhill, Vargas graduated from Boston University with degrees in political science and international relations. His early career included White House and U.S. Embassy internships focused on immigration, economy, and exports. ## Key Activities Vargas sponsors legislation on housing, zoning, public safety, civil service age waivers for Haverhill hires, and unemployment assistance. He emphasizes local approvals and community-focused bills.
