# CLB Hub Youth Engagement, Safety, and Employment Facilitation Policy (Level 2) **Version:** 0.9 (Draft) **Effective Date:** [Insert Date] **Applies To:** All CLB Hub users, providers, staff, and advisors/monitors --- ## 1. Purpose and Scope ### 1.1 Purpose This policy establishes binding rules for how CLB Hub enables youth career exploration and limited provider engagement while protecting minors’ safety and privacy. CLB Hub is designed to support local opportunity discovery and readiness while minimizing risk and ensuring appropriate safeguards. ### 1.2 Scope This policy applies to: - All CLB Hub users, including students, parents/guardians, providers, advisors/monitors, and CLB Hub staff - All provider listings, applications, and platform communications - All students under the age of 18, with restrictions by age tier - All data processed by CLB Hub relating to minors, parents/guardians, and providers ### 1.3 Policy Model This document implements **Level 2** controls: - **Users under 16:** browse and career preparation only, no provider contact or applications - **Users aged 16 to 17:** on-platform applications and messaging only, with adult oversight, no off-platform contact - **Users 18+:** adult terms (not covered in detail here; see CLB Hub Terms of Use) --- ## 2. Legal and Compliance Commitments ### 2.1 General Compliance CLB Hub and providers must comply with all applicable laws and regulations, including youth labor, privacy, safety, and nondiscrimination requirements. ### 2.2 Youth Employment Compliance (Provider Responsibility) Providers are solely responsible for complying with youth employment requirements, including employment permits and restrictions applicable to minors in Massachusetts. Massachusetts maintains statutory requirements and public guidance on minors’ employment permits and work restrictions. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0} ### 2.3 Data Security and Privacy (CLB Hub Responsibility) CLB Hub commits to implementing appropriate administrative, technical, and physical safeguards for personal information of Massachusetts residents, consistent with Massachusetts standards for protection of personal information. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1} ### 2.4 Children’s Online Privacy CLB Hub is not designed for children under 13. If CLB Hub becomes aware that it has collected personal information from a child under 13, it will take steps to remove the account and delete associated personal information, consistent with applicable requirements. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2} --- ## 3. Definitions For purposes of this policy: - **Student:** A user under age 18. - **Parent/Guardian:** A legal parent or guardian with responsibility for a student. - **Provider:** A business, nonprofit, school, or organization that lists opportunities on CLB Hub. - **Provider Admin:** A provider user authorized to create listings and respond to applications. - **Provider SME:** A provider user authorized to participate in provider operations related to a listing, as permitted by CLB Hub and the provider’s account configuration. - **Advisor/Monitor:** A designated adult user (for example guidance counselor, mentor, or CLB Hub staff) with oversight access to student activity and communications. - **Opportunity:** A provider listing, including jobs, internships, classes, camps, training programs, or events. - **Catalog:** The collection of a provider’s opportunities and related informational postings. - **Interest:** A student action that saves or bookmarks an opportunity. - **Application:** A student submission for an opportunity where allowed by age tier. - **Resume:** A career preparation document created by a student within CLB Hub. - **On-Platform Communication:** Messaging or interaction within CLB Hub systems. - **Off-Platform Communication:** Any communication outside CLB Hub, including email, phone, text, social media, external messaging apps, or in-person coordination initiated through CLB Hub. - **Personal Information:** Information that identifies or can reasonably be linked to an individual, including information about a student or parent/guardian. - **Minor Communication Log:** The retained record of on-platform communications involving a student. - **Incident:** A report or suspicion of unsafe conduct, boundary violations, harassment, exploitation, attempted off-platform contact, or other misconduct. --- ## 4. Platform Role and Limitations ### 4.1 CLB Hub’s Role CLB Hub functions as a facilitation platform for career exploration and limited supervised engagement. CLB Hub: - Is not an employer of students - Does not supervise students at worksites - Does not set wages, schedules, or job duties - Does not verify legal eligibility for a specific job or placement - Provides tools for discovery, readiness, and supervised on-platform engagement for ages 16 to 17 only ### 4.2 Provider Responsibilities Providers are solely responsible for: - Compliance with all child labor, wage, and hour laws - Work permits and eligibility verification where required - Safe supervision, training, and site safety - Screening, onboarding, and management of provider personnel who will interact with minors - Compliance with nondiscrimination requirements - Maintaining accurate and lawful listings Providers may not classify minors as independent contractors for work arranged through the provider. ### 4.3 Parent/Guardian Role Parents/guardians are responsible for: - Providing consent for age 16 to 17 participation as required by CLB Hub - Monitoring and supporting student participation - Reviewing communications as available through the platform - Escalating concerns promptly through reporting channels ### 4.4 Advisor/Monitor Role Advisors/monitors support safety and accountability by observing and escalating concerns. Their authority and duties are set out in Section 9. --- ## 5. Eligibility and Age-Based Participation Rules ### 5.1 Minimum Age CLB Hub is intended for students age 14 and older. Users under 13 are prohibited. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3} ### 5.2 Users Under Age 16 (Browse and Preparation Only) CLB Hub does not facilitate employment-related interactions for users under age 16. **Permitted Activities** - Browse provider catalogs and opportunity descriptions - View career pathways, requirements, and informational content - Save interests or favorites - Build resumes for educational and career preparation purposes - Participate in advisor-led or parent-guided exploration **Prohibited Activities** - Messaging providers - Applying or signing up for opportunities - Sharing resumes or application materials with providers - Interviews, scheduling, or coordination - Any off-platform contact initiated through CLB Hub ### 5.3 Users Aged 16 to 17 (Supervised On-Platform Engagement) Students aged 16 to 17 may participate in limited supervised engagement subject to the following requirements: - Parent/guardian consent is required before any provider messaging or applications - An advisor/monitor must be included in and have access to all communications involving the student - All communications must occur on-platform - Off-platform contact is prohibited under Level 2 (Section 8.3) --- ## 6. Provider Listings and Opportunity Controls ### 6.1 Listing Approval and Quality Controls CLB Hub may require provider listings to be reviewed and approved before publication. Review may include checks for: - Age requirements and suitability - Clarity of duties and supervision expectations - Paid versus unpaid status - Location and accessibility information - Prohibited environments or tasks ### 6.2 Age Gating and Display Rules Providers must specify minimum age eligibility for each opportunity. CLB Hub may automatically: - Hide “Apply” and “Message” functions for under-16 users - Display “Available at age 16+” or similar notices to under-16 users - Restrict visibility of provider contact details for minors ### 6.3 Prohibited Opportunities CLB Hub prohibits opportunities that involve: - Adult-only venues or environments - Hazardous activities or tasks prohibited for minors under applicable law - Unsupervised one-on-one worksite arrangements for minors without clear supervision - Work that requires a minor to operate a vehicle as part of the job - Roles that involve access to controlled substances, pornography, or illegal activities - Any role that requires off-platform coordination as a condition of application CLB Hub may maintain internal category and task restrictions to enforce these requirements. ### 6.4 Contractor and Small Business Listings CLB Hub may apply heightened scrutiny to listings from small contractors or small businesses, including: - Verification of a named on-site supervisor - Prohibition of informal “trial work” arrangements - Restrictions on duties, tools, and environments for minors - Additional documentation of insurance or safety controls --- ## 7. Resume and Career Preparation Tools ### 7.1 Resume Creation CLB Hub provides resume tools for career readiness. Resume content is subject to safety controls, including restrictions on contact information as described below. ### 7.2 Resume Visibility by Age Tier - **Under 16:** resumes are visible only to the student, parent/guardian, and advisors/monitors (if applicable). Providers cannot view under-16 resumes. - **Ages 16 to 17:** resumes may be shared with providers only on-platform and only in the context of an application, subject to monitoring and logging. ### 7.3 Contact Information Controls To reduce off-platform contact risk, CLB Hub may: - Prohibit students from including personal phone numbers, emails, or social media handles in resumes - Automatically redact or block contact information in resume fields - Prohibit providers from requesting contact information through the platform --- ## 8. Communications Policy (Level 2) ### 8.1 On-Platform Communications For communications involving minors: - All messages are logged and retained for a defined period (Section 11) - Advisors/monitors have access to the full thread history - CLB Hub may review communications to ensure compliance and safety - CLB Hub may restrict, pause, or terminate communications based on safety concerns ### 8.2 Communication Standards All users must comply with the following rules: - No harassment, discrimination, sexual content, grooming behavior, threats, or intimidation - No requests for secrecy - No exchange of personal contact information by or with minors - No requests for in-person meetings outside approved processes - No sharing of sensitive documents beyond what CLB Hub explicitly permits CLB Hub may maintain enforcement rules and detection measures to support these standards. ### 8.3 Off-Platform Communication Prohibited for Minors (Level 2 Rule) Off-platform communication is prohibited for all users under age 18. This includes: - Email, phone, SMS, WhatsApp, Signal, Telegram, Discord, and similar tools - Social media contact - Requests to “text me,” “call me,” or “email me” - Requests to meet in person arranged through CLB Hub communications Providers must not solicit off-platform contact from minors. Students must not attempt to initiate off-platform contact with providers. ### 8.4 Linking and External Redirects CLB Hub may restrict or disable external links in minor communications. If links are permitted in limited contexts, CLB Hub may require them to be informational only and not used to move communications off-platform. --- ## 9. Advisor/Monitor Oversight ### 9.1 Inclusion Requirements For all on-platform communications involving a student aged 16 to 17: - An advisor/monitor must be included in the thread, or have continuous access to the thread, as configured by CLB Hub - Parent/guardian view access may be provided and may be required by program rules ### 9.2 Authority Advisors/monitors and CLB Hub administrators may: - Flag content for review - Pause a conversation - Restrict a provider’s ability to message minors - Escalate incidents for investigation - Recommend suspension or removal of a provider ### 9.3 Training and Expectations Advisors/monitors should receive training that includes: - Recognizing boundary violations, grooming patterns, and coercion - Proper documentation of concerns - Escalation procedures and urgent response protocols - Appropriate youth-facing communication standards --- ## 10. Safety, Reporting, and Incident Response ### 10.1 Reporting Channels Users may report concerns through: - In-app reporting tools - A designated support email or hotline (to be specified) - Advisor/monitor escalation to CLB Hub administrators ### 10.2 Examples of Reportable Incidents Incidents include, but are not limited to: - Requests for off-platform contact - Requests for secrecy or deletion of messages - Sexual content or inappropriate comments - Threats, harassment, discrimination, or intimidation - Attempts to arrange unsupervised meetings - Suspicion of exploitation or trafficking - Unsafe work conditions described in listings or communications ### 10.3 Immediate Safety Actions CLB Hub may take immediate actions without prior notice, including: - Freezing messaging between a provider and minors - Suspending a provider account or listing - Suspending a student account if needed for safety - Notifying parent/guardian when warranted ### 10.4 Investigation and Documentation CLB Hub will: - Preserve relevant communication logs - Document actions taken and rationale - Coordinate with school partners or program officials when applicable - Apply consistent enforcement under Section 12 ### 10.5 Mandatory Reporting CLB Hub may have legal obligations to escalate certain concerns to appropriate authorities depending on the facts and the roles of involved individuals. Where legally required, CLB Hub will make or support required reports. --- ## 11. Data Privacy, Security, and Retention ### 11.1 Data Minimization CLB Hub collects and uses only the data reasonably necessary to: - Support account access and role assignment - Provide discovery and readiness tools - Enable supervised on-platform communications for ages 16 to 17 - Support safety monitoring and incident response ### 11.2 Access Controls CLB Hub uses role-based access controls to ensure: - Providers can access only what is necessary to review applications for eligible students - Under-16 student data is not exposed to providers for application purposes - Advisors/monitors have access to relevant student threads and records - Administrative access is limited to authorized personnel ### 11.3 Data Security Program CLB Hub maintains written and operational safeguards for personal information consistent with Massachusetts expectations for protection of personal information. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4} ### 11.4 Retention Schedule (Default) Unless otherwise required by law, program agreement, or active investigation, CLB Hub retains: - **Minor communication logs:** [Insert duration, for example 24 months] - **Applications and resume versions:** [Insert duration, for example 24 months or until deletion request] - **Provider verification artifacts:** [Insert duration, for example term of relationship plus 3 years] - **Incident records:** [Insert duration, for example 5 years] CLB Hub may extend retention when needed for safety, investigations, or legal compliance. ### 11.5 Parent/Guardian Rights Where applicable and as permitted by law, parents/guardians may request: - Access to student profile information - Correction of inaccurate information - Deletion of student information, subject to retention requirements for safety and compliance --- ## 12. Enforcement and Consequences ### 12.1 Provider Enforcement Provider violations may result in: - Listing edits required prior to approval - Warnings and mandatory corrective training - Messaging restrictions involving minors - Listing removal - Account suspension or termination - Referral to partner institutions where applicable ### 12.2 Student Enforcement Student violations may result in: - Education and guidance - Feature restrictions (for example disabling messaging) - Account suspension in serious cases - Parent/guardian notification where appropriate ### 12.3 Non-Retaliation Retaliation against any student, parent/guardian, advisor/monitor, or staff member for reporting concerns is prohibited and may result in removal from the platform. ### 12.4 Appeals CLB Hub may offer a review process for suspensions or removals. Final decision authority rests with CLB Hub administration. --- ## 13. Governance and Review ### 13.1 Ownership This policy is owned and maintained by CLB Hub administration. ### 13.2 Review Cycle This policy will be reviewed: - At least annually - After significant incidents - After material product changes affecting minors, communications, or data handling - After material legal or regulatory changes --- ## 14. Related Documents This policy should be read alongside: - Provider Agreement - Parent/Guardian Consent Form - Student Terms of Use - Code of Conduct - Privacy Policy - Data Security Program Summary (if maintained separately) --- ## Appendix A: Prohibited Conduct (Non-Exhaustive) - Any request for off-platform contact involving minors - Any request for secrecy or concealment from parents/guardians or advisors/monitors - Sexual content, sexualized comments, or romantic pursuit of minors - Harassment, discrimination, threats, or coercion - Attempts to schedule in-person meetings through CLB Hub for minors - Requests for money, gifts, or financial information from minors - Sharing illegal content or encouraging illegal activity --- ## Appendix B: Provider Listing Requirements (Minimum) Providers must include: - Opportunity title and description - Location and supervision expectations - Minimum age eligibility - Whether the role is paid or unpaid - Any prerequisites or required documentation - Contact must remain on-platform for minors under this Level 2 policy ---
