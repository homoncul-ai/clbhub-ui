# CLB Hub Youth Engagement, Safety, and Employment Facilitation Policy (Level 3) **Version:** 0.9 (Draft) **Effective Date:** [Insert Date] **Applies To:** All CLB Hub users, providers, staff, and advisors/monitors --- ## 1. Purpose and Scope ### 1.1 Purpose This policy establishes binding rules for how CLB Hub facilitates youth career exploration and supervised employment-related engagement, followed by a controlled transition to direct provider-student relationships. The goals of this policy are to: - Protect the safety and privacy of minors - Comply with applicable federal and Massachusetts laws - Clearly define and limit CLB Hub’s role in employment facilitation - Enable legitimate youth employment outcomes through a defined and documented handoff process ### 1.2 Scope This policy applies to: - All CLB Hub users, including students, parents/guardians, providers, advisors/monitors, and CLB Hub staff - All provider listings, applications, and communications facilitated by CLB Hub - All students under the age of 18, with restrictions by age tier - All data processed by CLB Hub relating to minors, parents/guardians, and providers ### 1.3 Policy Model This document implements **Level 3 (Controlled Handoff)** controls: - **Users under 16:** browse and career preparation only - **Users aged 16 to 17:** supervised on-platform applications and messaging, followed by a formal handoff - **Users 18+:** adult terms (not covered in detail here; see CLB Hub Terms of Use) --- ## 2. Legal and Compliance Commitments ### 2.1 General Compliance CLB Hub and providers must comply with all applicable laws and regulations, including youth labor, privacy, safety, and nondiscrimination requirements. ### 2.2 Youth Employment Compliance (Provider Responsibility) Providers are solely responsible for complying with all child labor and employment laws applicable to minors, including work permits, hour restrictions, prohibited tasks, wage and hour compliance, and workplace safety obligations. ### 2.3 Data Security and Privacy (CLB Hub Responsibility) CLB Hub commits to maintaining administrative, technical, and physical safeguards for personal information of Massachusetts residents, consistent with Massachusetts data protection standards. ### 2.4 Children’s Online Privacy CLB Hub is not designed for children under age 13. Accounts identified as belonging to children under 13 will be removed and associated data deleted in accordance with applicable requirements. --- ## 3. Definitions For purposes of this policy: - **Student:** A user under age 18. - **Parent/Guardian:** A legal parent or guardian responsible for a student. - **Provider:** A business, nonprofit, school, or organization that lists opportunities on CLB Hub. - **Provider Admin:** A provider user authorized to create listings and respond to applications. - **Provider SME:** A provider user authorized to participate in provider operations related to a listing. - **Advisor/Monitor:** A designated adult user (for example, guidance counselor, mentor, or CLB Hub staff) with oversight access to student activity and communications. - **Opportunity:** A provider listing, including jobs, internships, classes, camps, training programs, or events. - **Catalog:** The collection of a provider’s opportunities and informational postings. - **Interest:** A student action that saves or bookmarks an opportunity. - **Application:** A student submission for an opportunity where permitted. - **Resume:** A career preparation document created by a student within CLB Hub. - **On-Platform Communication:** Messaging or interaction conducted within CLB Hub systems. - **Off-Platform Communication:** Any communication occurring outside CLB Hub, including email, phone, text, social media, or in-person contact. - **Facilitation:** The act of enabling discovery, application, or supervised communication through CLB Hub. - **Placement:** A direct relationship between a student and a provider following acceptance. - **Acceptance:** A provider’s confirmation that a student has been selected for an opportunity. - **Formal Handoff:** The documented transition point at which CLB Hub ceases facilitation and responsibility shifts to the provider. --- ## 4. Platform Role and Limitations ### 4.1 CLB Hub’s Role CLB Hub functions as a facilitation platform that supports discovery, readiness, and supervised engagement prior to a defined handoff. CLB Hub: - Is not an employer or joint employer of students - Does not supervise students at worksites - Does not set wages, schedules, or job duties - Does not verify legal eligibility for specific jobs - Ends facilitation at the point of formal handoff ### 4.2 Provider Responsibilities Providers are solely responsible for: - Compliance with all child labor, wage, and hour laws - Obtaining and verifying work permits - Worksite safety, supervision, and training - Screening and management of provider personnel - Compliance with nondiscrimination laws Providers may not classify minors as independent contractors. ### 4.3 Parent/Guardian Role Parents/guardians are responsible for: - Providing consent for student participation - Reviewing acceptance and handoff acknowledgments - Monitoring student participation after handoff - Raising concerns through designated channels ### 4.4 Advisor/Monitor Role Advisors/monitors provide oversight during the facilitation phase only. Their authority and duties are defined in Section 9. --- ## 5. Eligibility and Age-Based Participation Rules ### 5.1 Minimum Age CLB Hub is intended for students age 14 and older. Users under age 13 are prohibited. ### 5.2 Users Under Age 16 (Browse and Preparation Only) CLB Hub does not facilitate employment-related interactions for users under age 16. **Permitted Activities** - Browse provider catalogs and opportunity descriptions - View career pathways and informational content - Save interests or favorites - Build resumes for educational and career preparation - Participate in advisor- or parent-guided exploration **Prohibited Activities** - Messaging providers - Applying or signing up for opportunities - Sharing resumes or application materials with providers - Interviews, scheduling, or coordination - Off-platform contact initiated through CLB Hub --- ### 5.3 Users Aged 16 to 17 (Supervised Facilitation + Handoff) Students aged 16 to 17 may participate in supervised facilitation subject to the following: - Parent/guardian consent is required - An advisor/monitor must have access to all on-platform communications - All pre-acceptance communications must occur on-platform - Off-platform contact is prohibited prior to formal handoff --- ## 6. Provider Listings and Opportunity Controls ### 6.1 Listing Review and Approval CLB Hub may review listings for: - Age eligibility - Safety and supervision expectations - Paid versus unpaid status - Prohibited environments or tasks ### 6.2 Age Gating CLB Hub may restrict features based on age, including disabling apply or message functions for under-16 users. ### 6.3 Prohibited Opportunities CLB Hub prohibits opportunities involving hazardous tasks, adult-only venues, illegal activity, or unsupervised work arrangements for minors. ### 6.4 Contractor and Small Business Controls Listings from contractors or small businesses may be subject to heightened review, including supervision requirements and task restrictions. --- ## 7. Resume and Application Handling ### 7.1 Resume Creation Resume tools are provided for career readiness. ### 7.2 Resume Visibility - **Under 16:** resumes are not visible to providers - **Ages 16 to 17:** resumes may be viewed by providers on-platform prior to acceptance ### 7.3 Resume Use After Handoff After formal handoff, providers may request or download resumes. CLB Hub does not control or supervise resume use after handoff. --- ## 8. Communications Policy (Level 3) ### 8.1 On-Platform Communications (Pre-Handoff) - All messages are logged and retained - Advisors/monitors have access to all threads - CLB Hub may intervene to pause or restrict communications ### 8.2 Communication Standards Prohibited conduct includes harassment, sexual content, grooming behavior, requests for secrecy, or attempts to move communications off-platform prior to handoff. ### 8.3 Off-Platform Communication (Post-Handoff Only) Off-platform communication is permitted only after all of the following conditions are met: - Provider acceptance - Parent/guardian consent - Provider acknowledgment of sole responsibility - System-recorded formal handoff Any off-platform communication prior to these steps is prohibited. --- ## 9. Advisor/Monitor Oversight ### 9.1 Scope of Oversight Advisor/monitor oversight applies only during the facilitation phase prior to handoff. ### 9.2 Authority Advisors/monitors and administrators may flag concerns, pause communications, and escalate incidents prior to handoff. ### 9.3 Post-Handoff Limitation After formal handoff, advisors/monitors no longer supervise or monitor provider-student communications. --- ## 10. Safety, Reporting, and Incident Response ### 10.1 Reporting Channels Concerns may be reported through in-app tools or designated support channels. ### 10.2 Immediate Actions CLB Hub may suspend facilitation, freeze communications, or revoke listings during investigation. ### 10.3 Mandatory Reporting Where legally required, CLB Hub will escalate concerns to appropriate authorities. --- ## 11. Data Privacy, Security, and Retention ### 11.1 Data Minimization and Access Controls Access to student data is limited by role and age tier. ### 11.2 Retention CLB Hub retains pre-handoff communications and records for defined periods. Post-handoff interactions are not logged or retained by CLB Hub. ### 11.3 Parent Rights Parents/guardians may request access or correction of student data retained by CLB Hub, subject to retention obligations. --- ## 12. Enforcement and Consequences ### 12.1 Provider Enforcement Violations may result in listing removal, account suspension, or termination. ### 12.2 Student Enforcement Violations may result in education, feature restrictions, or account suspension. ### 12.3 Non-Retaliation Retaliation for reporting concerns is prohibited. --- ## 13. Governance and Review ### 13.1 Ownership This policy is maintained by CLB Hub administration. ### 13.2 Review Cycle Reviewed annually or after significant incidents or platform changes. --- ## 14. Related Documents This policy should be read alongside: - Provider Agreement - Parent/Guardian Consent Form - Student Terms of Use - Code of Conduct - Privacy Policy ---
