# CLB Hub Youth Engagement, Safety, and Employment Facilitation Policy ## 1. Purpose and Scope ### 1.1 Purpose This policy establishes the rules, safeguards, and limitations governing how CLB Hub facilitates youth career exploration and employment-related interactions. Its goals are to: - Protect the safety and privacy of minors - Comply with applicable federal and Massachusetts laws - Clearly allocate responsibilities among CLB Hub, providers, parents/guardians, and advisors - Enable legitimate youth career exploration and workforce development while minimizing risk ### 1.2 Scope This policy applies to: - All users of the CLB Hub platform - All providers that list opportunities or interact with students - All communications facilitated by CLB Hub - All students under the age of 18, with additional requirements based on age --- ## 2. Definitions For purposes of this policy: - **Student**: A user of CLB Hub who is under the age of 18. - **Parent / Guardian**: A legal parent or guardian responsible for a student. - **Provider**: Any business, nonprofit, school, or organization that lists opportunities on CLB Hub. - **Advisor / Monitor**: A designated adult user (such as a guidance counselor, mentor, or CLB Hub staff member) with oversight access to student activity and communications. - **Opportunity**: A listing created by a provider, including jobs, internships, classes, camps, or training programs. - **Application**: A student’s submission of interest or materials in response to an opportunity. - **Resume**: A career preparation document created by a student within the platform. - **On-Platform Communication**: Messaging or interaction conducted within CLB Hub’s systems. - **Off-Platform Communication**: Any communication occurring outside CLB Hub, including email, phone, text, or in-person contact. - **Facilitation**: The act of enabling discovery, application, or communication through CLB Hub. - **Placement**: The establishment of a direct relationship between a student and a provider. - **Acceptance**: A provider’s confirmation that a student has been selected for an opportunity. - **Formal Handoff**: The defined moment when CLB Hub ends facilitation and responsibility shifts fully to the provider (where applicable). --- ## 3. Platform Role and Limitations ### 3.1 CLB Hub’s Role CLB Hub operates as a facilitation platform for career exploration and limited employment-related interactions. CLB Hub: - Is not an employer or joint employer of students - Does not supervise students at worksites - Does not set wages, schedules, or job duties - Does not guarantee placements or outcomes - Facilitates discovery and limited communication only as defined in this policy ### 3.2 Provider Responsibilities Providers are solely responsible for: - Compliance with all applicable child labor, wage, and hour laws - Obtaining and verifying required work permits - Supervision, training, and safety of students - Compliance with nondiscrimination and workplace safety laws Providers may not misclassify students as independent contractors. ### 3.3 Parent and Guardian Role Parents and guardians are responsible for: - Providing consent where required - Monitoring student participation - Acknowledging the scope and limits of CLB Hub’s role - Engaging with advisors or CLB Hub staff when concerns arise --- ## 4. Age-Based Participation Rules ### 4.1 Users Under Age 16 CLB Hub does not facilitate employment-related interactions for users under the age of 16. **Permitted Activities** - Browsing provider catalogs - Viewing opportunity descriptions and career pathways - Saving interests or favorites - Building resumes for educational and career preparation purposes - Advisor- or parent-guided exploration **Prohibited Activities** - Messaging providers - Applying or signing up for opportunities - Sharing resumes with providers - Off-platform communication with providers - Scheduling, coordination, or interviews --- ### 4.2 Users Aged 16–17 Students aged 16–17 may participate in limited, supervised employment-related interactions subject to the following requirements: - Parental consent is required - An advisor or monitor must have access to all communications - All pre-acceptance communications must occur on-platform - Off-platform contact is prohibited prior to acceptance --- ## 5. Communications Policy ### 5.1 On-Platform Communications - All on-platform communications are logged and retained for a defined period - Advisors or monitors have access to communications involving minors - CLB Hub may review communications to ensure compliance and safety - CLB Hub reserves the right to pause, restrict, or terminate communications ### 5.2 Off-Platform Communications **Level 2 Option** - Off-platform communication is prohibited for all users under age 18 **Level 3 Option** - Off-platform communication for users aged 16–17 is permitted only after: - Provider acceptance - Parental consent - Provider acknowledgment of sole responsibility - Formal handoff by CLB Hub Off-platform communication prior to meeting these conditions is prohibited. --- ## 6. Resume and Application Handling ### 6.1 Resume Creation - Resume tools are provided for career readiness and preparation - Resumes may be viewed by students, parents, and advisors - Personal contact information may be restricted or redacted ### 6.2 Resume Sharing - Users under 16 may not share resumes with providers - Users aged 16–17 may share resumes with providers on-platform only - Resume downloading or external use is prohibited prior to acceptance ### 6.3 Post-Acceptance Resume Use - After formal handoff, providers may request or download resumes - CLB Hub is no longer responsible for resume use after handoff --- ## 7. Provider Eligibility and Restrictions ### 7.1 General Requirements Providers must: - Complete identity and organizational verification - Agree to CLB Hub policies and terms - Maintain accurate and truthful listings ### 7.2 Additional Requirements for Contractors and Small Businesses - Heightened review may apply - Certain task categories may be prohibited - Paid placements may be required - Proof of supervision and insurance may be required ### 7.3 Prohibited Opportunities CLB Hub prohibits listings involving: - Hazardous or unsafe environments - Adult-only venues - Tasks prohibited for minors under applicable law --- ## 8. Advisor / Monitor Role ### 8.1 Authority Advisors and monitors may: - View communications involving minors - Pause or restrict interactions - Escalate concerns to CLB Hub administrators ### 8.2 Training Advisors and monitors should receive training on: - Youth safety and boundaries - Recognizing concerning behavior - Escalation and documentation procedures --- ## 9. Safety, Escalation, and Incident Response ### 9.1 Reporting Concerns Students, parents, providers, and advisors may report concerns through designated channels. ### 9.2 Incident Handling CLB Hub may: - Suspend provider or student access - Investigate incidents - Notify parents or guardians as appropriate ### 9.3 Mandatory Reporting Where legally required, CLB Hub will escalate concerns to appropriate authorities. --- ## 10. Data Privacy and Retention ### 10.1 Data Use - Data is collected solely to support platform functionality - Access is role-based and limited ### 10.2 Retention - Communications, applications, and resumes are retained for defined periods - Data is deleted in accordance with platform policies ### 10.3 Parental Rights Parents may request access, correction, or deletion of student data as permitted by law. --- ## 11. Enforcement and Consequences ### 11.1 Provider Violations Violations may result in: - Warnings - Suspension - Termination of access ### 11.2 Student Violations Responses may include: - Education and guidance - Access restrictions ### 11.3 Appeals Disputes may be reviewed by CLB Hub administration. --- ## 12. Governance and Review ### 12.1 Policy Ownership This policy is maintained by CLB Hub administration. ### 12.2 Review Cycle This policy is reviewed annually or following significant incidents. --- ## 13. Related Documents This policy should be read alongside: - Provider Agreement - Parent Consent Form - Student Terms of Use - Code of Conduct - Privacy Policy ---
