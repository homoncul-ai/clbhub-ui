# CLB Hub Policy Options ## Comparison of Level 2 and Level 3 Youth Engagement Models This document describes the operational, policy, and legal differences between **Level 2 (High Conservatism)** and **Level 3 (Controlled Handoff)** as defined in the CLB Hub Youth Engagement, Safety, and Employment Facilitation Policy. It is intended to support internal decision-making, partner discussions, and legal review. --- ## 1. Overview of Policy Levels ### Level 2: High Conservatism CLB Hub facilitates discovery, applications, and **on-platform-only communication** for students aged 16–17. No off-platform contact is permitted for any user under age 18. ### Level 3: Controlled Handoff CLB Hub facilitates discovery, applications, and on-platform communication for students aged 16–17, followed by a **formal handoff** to the provider after acceptance and parental consent. Off-platform contact is permitted only after the handoff. --- ## 2. Summary Comparison Table | Area | Level 2 | Level 3 | |-----|--------|--------| | Under 16 participation | Browse only | Browse only | | 16–17 applications | Allowed | Allowed | | On-platform messaging | Required | Required (pre-handoff) | | Off-platform contact | Prohibited | Allowed post-handoff | | Resume download | Prohibited | Allowed post-handoff | | Advisor oversight | Continuous | Continuous pre-handoff | | CLB Hub facilitation end | Never | At formal handoff | | Provider responsibility shift | Partial | Explicit and documented | | Legal risk exposure | Lowest | Moderate but controlled | --- ## 3. Detailed Policy Differences ### 3.1 Communications #### Level 2 - All communications involving minors must occur on-platform. - No exchange of phone numbers, emails, or external links. - CLB Hub retains visibility and oversight for the full duration of interactions. - Providers are prohibited from initiating or requesting off-platform contact. #### Level 3 - All pre-acceptance communications must occur on-platform. - After acceptance and parental consent, CLB Hub formally ends facilitation. - Off-platform communication is permitted only after: - Provider acceptance - Parent or guardian consent - Provider acknowledgment of sole responsibility - System-recorded formal handoff --- ### 3.2 Resume Handling #### Level 2 - Resumes may be created by all students. - Under 16: resumes are never visible to providers. - Ages 16–17: resumes may be viewed by providers on-platform only. - Downloading, exporting, or external use of resumes is prohibited. #### Level 3 - Same restrictions as Level 2 prior to handoff. - After formal handoff: - Providers may download or request resumes. - Resume use occurs outside CLB Hub systems. - CLB Hub disclaims responsibility for post-handoff use. --- ### 3.3 Role of CLB Hub #### Level 2 - CLB Hub continuously facilitates and supervises interactions. - CLB Hub remains the communication intermediary at all times. - Higher operational oversight obligation. - No clear termination of facilitation role. #### Level 3 - CLB Hub facilitates until a defined endpoint. - After handoff, CLB Hub no longer: - Facilitates communication - Oversees provider-student interaction - Participates in scheduling or coordination - Provider assumes full responsibility post-handoff. --- ## 4. Legal Risk Analysis ### 4.1 Level 2 Legal Risk Profile **Risk Characteristics** - Minimal child labor exposure - No off-platform grooming risk - Reduced wage and hour exposure - Strong alignment with school-based models **Primary Legal Posture** - CLB Hub functions as a supervised facilitation platform. - CLB Hub retains duty of care due to continuous involvement. - Lower likelihood of disputes over role ambiguity. **Key Tradeoff** - Ongoing oversight obligations - Greater responsibility for monitoring communications - Potential claims tied to failure to intervene despite visibility --- ### 4.2 Level 3 Legal Risk Profile **Risk Characteristics** - Increased exposure after handoff due to real-world employment relationships - Risk concentrated at the clarity of the handoff - Greater importance of documentation and consent **Primary Legal Posture** - CLB Hub is not the employer or placement agency post-handoff. - Responsibility shifts explicitly to the provider. - Risk is reduced if the handoff boundary is clear and enforced. **Key Tradeoff** - Slightly higher complexity - Requires strong contracts and system enforcement - Allows scalable placements with manageable risk --- ## 5. Litigation and Regulatory Considerations ### Level 2 - Easier to defend as a continuous, supervised environment. - Strong appeal to school districts and insurers. - Lower likelihood of child labor or wage claims. ### Level 3 - Defensible if handoff is clear, recorded, and consented. - More aligned with real employment outcomes. - Requires disciplined execution to avoid role confusion. --- ## 6. Insurance and Partner Optics ### Level 2 - Viewed favorably by: - School districts - Municipal partners - Youth-serving insurers - Lower premiums and fewer underwriting questions. ### Level 3 - Generally acceptable with: - Clear policy language - Provider agreements - Parent consent documentation - May require additional insurer review. --- ## 7. Operational Impact ### Level 2 - Simpler system logic - Higher advisor workload - Slower placement velocity ### Level 3 - More complex workflows - Clear lifecycle transitions - Enables faster and more scalable placements --- ## 8. Decision Guidance Choose **Level 2** if: - Primary goal is exploration and preparation - Institutional partners prioritize safety above placements - Operational capacity for monitoring is high Choose **Level 3** if: - Goal includes real employment outcomes - Providers expect direct engagement post-acceptance - CLB Hub can enforce clean handoffs and documentation --- ## 9. Recommendation Summary Both Level 2 and Level 3 are legally defensible if implemented correctly. - Level 2 minimizes legal exposure but limits placement outcomes. - Level 3 balances opportunity with risk through a defined handoff. The critical determinant of risk is not the level chosen, but whether the platform enforces the boundaries defined by that level. ---
