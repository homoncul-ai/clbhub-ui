# Haverhill Helps ## Overview **Haverhill Helps** (haverhillhelps.com) is a community mutual aid platform serving Haverhill, Massachusetts residents facing challenges like SNAP benefit cuts and federal program reductions. It facilitates anonymous, safe peer-to-peer interactions for requesting or offering local support. ## Core Purpose The site builds direct community connections to provide assistance during economic difficulties, reducing dependence on government programs. ## Main Features - Anonymous posting of help requests and offers - Focus on resident-led, local support networks
