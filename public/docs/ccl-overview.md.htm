# CCL Hub: Connecting Communities Locally ## Overview **CCL Hub** is a community-centered digital platform designed to map and activate the ecosystem of a town or region. Its core purpose is to help residents—especially students—connect with local institutions, businesses, and nonprofits to access opportunities, services, and vocational programs. The platform promotes collaboration between individuals and local providers by making the town’s social and economic network visible and interactive online. ## Initial Mission: Addressing the Vocational School Bottleneck The project’s first focus is tackling Massachusetts’ significant backlog of students seeking admission to vocational schools. **CCL Hub** aims to bridge that gap by connecting students, schools, and training providers so that young people can discover, apply to, and prepare for local programs that align with their interests and career goals. ## Key Roles and Participants The platform supports multiple user roles to model real community relationships: - **Clients (Students and Families):** Students use the Hub to explore local opportunities, build resumes, and express career interests. - **Providers (Schools, Colleges, Businesses, Nonprofits):** Providers maintain catalogs of offerings—such as classes, internships, events, or services—that students can browse and apply for. - **Advisors (Guidance Counselors and Mentors):** Advisors oversee student progress, engage in secure chat discussions, and monitor work requests for safety and accountability. ## Core Features 1. **Community Directory and Onboarding:** The Hub captures the digital presence of local organizations and institutions, enabling students to easily find verified local providers. Schools facilitate onboarding for students and families. 2. **Provider Catalogs:** Each provider maintains a catalog of offerings (courses, jobs, events, or services). Students can browse these and submit “work requests” (tickets) to engage directly with providers. 6. **Personal Goals and Interest Matching:** Students set up personal goals (e.g., “I want to be a plumber”) that serve as context for personalized search and matching. The system uses vocational classifications to suggest relevant local opportunities aligned with those interests. 3. ** Showing Interest ** A student finds offerings either by searching, or by a presentation through some kind of persona "Feed". The offerings are presented with a thumbs up button. Clicking thumbs up (I like this) creates an "Interest" that the student wants save this for further pursuit. This interest model allows kids to quickly scroll through and choose many offerings to be interested in. 3. **Work Request System:** After the Interest stage students can proceed to the signup stage. They can see the requirement in the "Signup Packet" prepare and present a resume, and fill out any forms. Upon completing the signup process, workrequest (ticket) is created and routed to the provider. Provider agents can review student profiles, resumes, and transcripts before responding. Ticket types can vary—from course enrollment to resource requests like transportation or school materials. 4. **Communication and Safety:** A built-in group chat system, modeled after Slack, allows students to communicate securely with providers and advisors. Conversations are transparent to the advisory team to ensure compliance and safety, especially for students under 18. 5. **Student Profiles and Resume Builder:** Students can build goal-specific resumes tailored to vocational interests, aiding in applications and interactions with providers. 6. ** Tracking Progress towards Personal Goals ** Each personal goal the student sets up will have an advisory team set up. This team will include guidance, parents, mentors, and the student. 7. **Analytics and Reporting:** Providers gain insight into community needs—e.g., which programs or careers attract the most interest—helping them shape future offerings that better serve local demand. Students gain insight by being able to see a stats around the programs. 8. ** General Research ** In addition to being a work request system to match, we also want to curate relevant information for careers that can be maintained by the community. We can use AI for the first cut of all this information, but we will also have the means to save and forward "Information Packets" that are curated by Advocates and Providers. ## Philosophy: The Local Ecosystem At the heart of **CCL Hub** is the idea of locality—anchoring digital engagement to real communities. By focusing on local access and proximity, the platform ensures that opportunities are practical and attainable for students. This ecosystem approach aims to strengthen community ties, enhance equity in educational access, and empower youth to navigate their career paths within a network of supportive local partners. This is not meant to be a for-profit engagement, and we're just trying to solve some problems. We pledge and require that the data from this enterprise will never be sold.
