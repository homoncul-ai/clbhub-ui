# Meeting: Vargas/Haverhill Helps and CCL Hub ## Purpose We're meeting to discuss synergies between the **CCL Hub** application and the **Haverhill Helps** application. Each of us has a similar idea of "Ecosystem." CCL Hub's first ecosystem will be Haverhill. We might be able to help each other out. ## Potential Collaboration - **CCL Hub** providing the backend, registration, onboarding, and catalog management - **Haverhill Helps** providing the frontend and the boots on the ground to work with the "Providers" ## Expanded Model CCL Hub can be expanded from a student/provider model into a more general **Client/Provider** model that would allow citizens access to the ecosystem in one of two ways: 1. **Direct Communication:** Work requests between client (citizen) and provider (non-profits, state agencies, etc.) 2. **Advocate-Based Communication:** A client (citizen) has an advocate (case worker) help getting access to services This meeting is to explore a relationship. ## Presentation Outline (7-10 slides) 1. Who CCL Hub is and what it does 2. Commonalities between CCL Hub and Haverhill Helps (Vargas) 4. CCL Hub work ticket as used by Haverhill Helps for their purposes 5. CCL Hub benefitting from having help getting providers on board 6. How does haverhill helps define success? 7. Haverhill Helps challenges 8. Next steps ## Presentation Details ### 1. Who CCL Hub Is and What It Does - Community-centered digital platform designed to map and activate a town's ecosystem - Connects residents (especially students) with local institutions, businesses, and nonprofits - Core features: provider catalogs, work request ticketing system, slack-like messaging, resume builder - Primary focus: addressing Massachusetts' vocational school admission backlog with jobs, educational opportunities, and internships. - Philosophy: locality-first approach anchoring digital engagement to real communities - Business Model: Build something of value for the community and then find a way to sustain it. ### 2. Commonalities Between CCL Hub and Haverhill Helps (Vargas) - Both centered on the concept of a local "Ecosystem" - Both aim to connect residents with resources and services - Shared geography: Haverhill as the pilot community - Community-first, non-profit orientation - Focus on reducing barriers to access for underserved populations ### 3. Haverhill Helps benefits from CCL Hub system - Work requests serve as structured communication between clients and providers - Ticket lifecycle: Interest → Signup → Work Request → Provider Response - Work requests can be adapted for mutual aid requests (food, transportation, housing assistance) - Built-in tracking of work requests and accountability - Electronic records of work requests and provider responses provide metrics based analysis of the ecosystem ### 4. CCL Hub Benefits from Provider Onboarding Help - Haverhill Helps has existing relationships with local non-profits and service providers - Knowledge of the community to identify and recruit providers - Local credibility and trust already established - Knowledge of which organizations are most active and responsive - Can help translate CCL Hub's student-focused model to broader community services - Know-how of city/state resources and how to access them ### 5. How Does Haverhill Helps Define Success? - Number of residents helped / requests fulfilled? - Provider participation and engagement levels? - Community awareness and adoption? - Reduction in unmet needs? - Sustainability of the platform long-term? ### 6. Haverhill Helps Challenges - Volunteer capacity and burnout - Funding and sustainability - Technology infrastructure needs - Provider recruitment and retention - Balancing anonymity with accountability - Scaling while maintaining community feel ### 7. Next Steps - Define pilot scope and timeline - Identify initial provider partners - Technical integration requirements - Roles and responsibilities agreement - Success metrics and checkpoints - Follow-up meeting schedule
